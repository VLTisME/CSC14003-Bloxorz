## Visual Design & Aesthetics
- **Inspiration**: Stardew Valley (ConcernedApe)
- **Palette**: Custom 29-color curated pixel art palette based on warm earthy tones, parchment neutrals, and lush meadow greens.
- **Components**:
  - Procedural 9-slice wooden buttons with 4-step ramps (Normal, Hover, Pressed, Selected).
  - Procedural parchment panel dialogs and stage select tiles with wooden bevel borders.
  - Procedural dual-tone logo with 1px ink outline and 2px drop shadow.
  - Procedural meadow grass, cracked fragile planks, timber bridge, and rune switch tiles.
